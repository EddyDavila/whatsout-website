GOOD TO KNOW

Clear answers.
No big promises.

Can I download What’s Out today?

The Android release is under Google Play review. Public availability is not yet confirmed. You can explore the web experience or contact us about the launch; no subscription is sold on this website.

Are all nearby events sent as push notifications?

No. The closest-party feature is an in-app prompt based on loaded results. Eligible VIP invitations have a notification workflow, but What’s Out does not promise a background alert for every nearby event.

What does a business subscription change?

It supports eligible event publication alongside business-management tools. Event Mode separates event discovery from general places. It does not guarantee exclusive placement or customer traffic. Supported one-event purchase options may also be available; check current terms in the app.

Are searches instant or complete everywhere?

No. Loading and freshness vary with network, device, location density, available data, and service status. Only supported cities, categories, and available listings are shown.

An agent introduced me. How do I keep their referral?

Use that agent’s personal Business Owner QR, sign into your verified account, and wait for the saved-referral confirmation before buying an eligible business subscription with the same account. A scan alone does not save a referral.
